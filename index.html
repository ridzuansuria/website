About us





WHO ARE WE?

RIDZUAN SURIA SDN.BHD is a business involved in generating, manufacturing, installing, or servicing solar energy systems that convert sunlight into electricity or thermal energy. These companies play a central role in the global transition toward renewable energy







Our Mission statement



For Residential Solar Installers:

"To empower homeowners with clean, affordable, and independent solar energy, making renewable power simple, accessible, and rewarding for every household."

For Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Solar Developers:
"To drive corporate sustainability and operational savings by designing and deploying scalable, high-performance solar energy systems for forward-thinking businesses."

For Technology & Manufacturing Solar Firms:

To pioneer cutting-edge photovoltaic technology and battery storage solutions that maximize energy capture, improve efficiency, and advance grid reliability worldwide."
